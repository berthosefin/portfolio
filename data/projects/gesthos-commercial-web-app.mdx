# Gesthos Commercial

Complete, centralized commercial management system designed for small and
medium businesses that want to digitalize their sales processes.

## Features

- **Partners (Clients & Suppliers)**: Unified records with full transaction history
- **Product & Service Catalog**: Centralized repository with multi-VAT handling
- **Purchasing & Supply**: Purchase orders, goods receipts, supplier invoices
- **Sales & Quotations**: Quotes, orders, delivery notes, customer invoicing
- **Inventory Management**: Multi-warehouse, FIFO/LIFO, low-stock alerts
- **Cash Register & Payments**: Collections, disbursements, reconciliation
- **Dashboard & Reports**: Real-time KPIs, PDF/Excel exports

## Tech Stack

- TanStack Start
- TypeScript
- Prisma (PostgreSQL)
- shadcn/ui
- Better Auth
- TanStack Query
- Zod
- Recharts
