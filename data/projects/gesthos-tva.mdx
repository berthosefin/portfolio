# Gesthos TVA

Web application for VAT management that lets businesses handle their purchase
and sales invoices, monitor tax obligations, and file VAT declarations in a
structured, fully traceable way.

## Why Gesthos TVA exists

For many small businesses, VAT season means spreadsheets: collected and
deductible amounts reconciled by hand, declarations rebuilt from memory, and
past filings buried in folders. Gesthos TVA turns that into a single workflow
where every declaration traces back to its source documents — and survives an
audit.

## Features

- **Accounting Documents**: Purchase/sales entries with automatic VAT & TTC computation
- **4-step Declaration Flow**: Selection, adjustment, summary, validation
- **Dashboard & Statistics**: KPIs, monthly trends, declaration history table
- **Legal Archiving**: Filed declarations close their period and stay retrievable
- **FEC Export**: Generates a Fichier des Écritures Comptables, the legally required format for accountants and auditors
- **Multi-company**: Admin manages companies and users; users see their own data only
- **CSV/PDF Export**: Export documents and generate declaration PDFs

## The declaration flow

| Step | Focus | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Selection | Gather the period's purchase and sales documents | Complete document set |
| 2 · Adjustment | Reconcile VAT collected vs deductible, correct entries | Validated amounts |
| 3 · Summary | Review totals per VAT rate before filing | Declaration preview |
| 4 · Validation | Lock the period and file | Archived declaration + PDF |

Each validated declaration keeps its link to the underlying documents, so the
answer to "where does this number come from?" is always two clicks away.

## Built for compliance

Closed periods are archived rather than deleted, and the FEC export produces
the standard accounting file that chartered accountants expect. Accounts are
provisioned by an administrator instead of open self-signup, which keeps every
company's fiscal data isolated behind the multi-company model.
