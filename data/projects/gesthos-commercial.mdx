# Gesthos Commercial

Complete, centralized commercial management system designed for small and
medium businesses that want to digitalize their sales processes.

## Why Gesthos Commercial exists

Quotes live in Word, stock lives in a spreadsheet, receipts live in a
notebook — and nobody can answer "what did we actually sell this month?"
without an afternoon of reconstruction. Gesthos Commercial puts the whole
chain in one place, so a sale entered once flows through delivery, invoicing,
inventory, and reporting on its own.

## Features

- **Partners (Clients & Suppliers)**: Unified records with full transaction history
- **Product & Service Catalog**: Centralized repository with multi-VAT handling
- **Purchasing & Supply**: Purchase orders, goods receipts, supplier invoices
- **Sales & Quotations**: Quotes, orders, delivery notes, customer invoicing, credit notes
- **Inventory Management**: Multi-warehouse, FIFO/LIFO valuation, low-stock alerts
- **Cash Register & Payments**: Collections, disbursements, reconciliation
- **Dashboard & Reports**: Real-time KPIs, PDF/Excel exports

## From quote to payment

Every document in the sales chain knows its neighbors, so progress is tracked
without re-entering data:

| Document | Direction | Role in the chain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quotation | Sales | Offer sent to the customer, convertible into an order |
| Sales order | Sales | Confirmed deal that drives fulfillment |
| Delivery note | Sales | Goods shipped, evidence of delivery |
| Invoice | Sales | Amount due, feeds revenue KPIs |
| Credit note | Sales | Correction after a return or discount |
| Purchase order | Purchasing | Goods ordered from a supplier |
| Goods receipt | Purchasing | Stock in, checked against PO and invoice |
| Supplier invoice | Purchasing | Payable registered for payment |

## Stock and cash, kept honest

Inventory is valued per warehouse with FIFO or LIFO, and low-stock alerts
surface what needs reordering before it blocks a sale. On the cash side,
collections and disbursements are recorded as they happen and reconciled, so
the register matches reality at the end of the day.

## Access model

Like Gesthos TVA, accounts are provisioned by an administrator rather than
open self-signup — each business gets an isolated environment configured for
its own partners, catalog, and warehouses.
