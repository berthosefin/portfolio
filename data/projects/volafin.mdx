# VolaFin

Personal finance management platform built around one question: how much can
I actually spend? *Vola* means money in Malagasy — the rest is FINance.

## Why VolaFin exists

A bank balance is a liar by omission: it shows what you have, not what is
already spoken for. Rent due Friday, the subscription renewing next week —
money that looks available and isn't. VolaFin computes a Real Available
Balance in real time so the number you see is the number you can spend.

## The Real Available Balance

Gross balances across every account, minus everything already committed to
upcoming charges:

| | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Gross balance (all accounts) | 2 180 000 Ar |
| Committed (upcoming recurring charges) | -939 500 Ar |
| **Real available balance** | **1 240 500 Ar** |

The figure updates live as transactions and schedules change — no month-end
surprise, no mental arithmetic.

## Features

- **Multi-Account Management**: Bank accounts, cash, Mobile Money — one unified dashboard
- **Real Available Balance**: What you can truly spend after committed charges (see above)
- **Income & Expenses Tracking**: Categorized transactions
- **Recurring Expenses**: Schedules generated and tracked automatically
- **Financial Forecasts**: Balance evolution charts that surface tight months before they happen
- **Savings Goals**: Set targets, track contributions, celebrate completion
- **Admin Dashboard**: Full control over finances
- **Enhanced Security**: Secure authentication, encrypted tokens, private data

## In your pocket, not just your browser

VolaFin ships as an Android app distributed through versioned GitHub
Releases (currently v1.3.0) — installable without any app store — alongside
the web application. The interface is bilingual English/French thanks to
next-intl.
